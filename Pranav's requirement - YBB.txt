Lesson titles and LMS descriptions

Lesson #
Lesson Title
Description
1
What Is Business Broking?
Understand what business broking is, how brokers create value, and where they fit in a business-sale transaction.
2
Role of a Business Broker
Learn the broker’s role from sourcing opportunities to managing buyers, negotiations and transaction progress.
3
Business Broking Industry in India
Explore the opportunity, current market landscape and long-term potential of professional business broking in India.
4
Types of Business Transactions
Understand common transaction types, including business sales, asset sales, share sales, mergers and acquisitions.
5
Understanding Different Business Models
Learn how businesses create, deliver and capture value through different operating and revenue models.
6
SME vs Startup vs Corporate
Distinguish between SMEs, startups and corporates, and understand how each differs as a potential transaction opportunity.
7
Business Life Cycle
Identify the key stages of a business life cycle and how growth stage affects sale readiness and buyer interest.
8
Why Owners Sell Businesses
Understand the financial, personal and strategic reasons why business owners decide to sell.
9
Code of Conduct for Business Brokers
Learn the professional standards, integrity principles and behaviours expected from an Authorised Business Broker.
10
Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)
Understand why confidentiality matters and how NDAs protect sellers, buyers and sensitive transaction information.
11
Conflict of Interest & Ethical Decision Making
Learn how to identify conflicts of interest and make fair, transparent and professional decisions.
12
Professional Communication & Client Relationship Management
Build strong client relationships through clear communication, responsiveness, trust and expectation management.
13
Prospecting Techniques
Learn practical methods to identify and approach potential business sellers.
14
Networking Strategy
Understand how to build a focused network that generates consistent business-sale opportunities.
15
Working with Referrals
Learn how to create referral relationships and convert introductions into qualified seller opportunities.
16
Building Seller Relationships
Develop the trust, credibility and advisory approach needed to work effectively with business owners.
17
Types of Buyers
Understand strategic buyers, financial buyers, individual buyers, HNIs, family offices and other buyer categories.
18
Investor Psychology
Learn what motivates buyers, how they assess risk and what increases their confidence in a transaction.
19
Buyer Qualification
Learn how to assess a buyer’s financial capacity, intent, experience and suitability before sharing confidential information.
20
Managing Buyer Expectations
Understand how to set realistic expectations around valuation, timelines, confidentiality and deal certainty.
21
Introduction to Business Valuation
Learn the purpose of valuation and the key factors that influence what a business may be worth.
22
Asset-Based Valuation
Understand how assets, liabilities and net asset value can be used to estimate business value.
23
Income-Based Valuation
Learn how earnings, cash flow and future income potential influence valuation.
24
Market-Based Valuation
Understand comparable transactions, industry benchmarks and valuation multiples.
25
Understanding EBITDA, SDE & Cash Flow
Learn the core financial measures used by buyers and brokers to assess business performance.
26
Valuation Multiples by Industry
Understand why valuation multiples vary across industries, business models, growth profiles and risk levels.
27
Valuation Mistakes & Seller Expectation Management
Learn how to avoid unrealistic valuations and guide sellers toward credible expectations.
28
What Is a Mandate?
Understand the purpose of a business-sale mandate and why it is essential before representing a seller.
29
Exclusive vs Non-Exclusive Mandates
Compare exclusive and non-exclusive mandates, including their advantages, risks and suitable use cases.
30
Creating a Business Sale Mandate Agreement
Learn the essential terms to include in a professional broker mandate agreement.
31
Information Gathering & Seller Onboarding
Learn how to collect business, financial, operational and strategic information from a seller.
32
Creating a Confidential Business Profile (CBP)
Understand how to prepare a professional profile that presents a business clearly while protecting confidential information.
33
Creating Anonymous Business Teasers
Learn how to create short, attractive buyer teasers without revealing the seller’s identity.
34
NDA & Confidentiality Management
Learn how to manage NDA execution, controlled information sharing and confidentiality throughout the buyer process.
35
Building a Buyer Database
Learn how to create, organise and maintain a targeted database of potential business buyers.
36
Building Referral Networks with CAs, Bankers & Stock Brokers
Understand how professional intermediaries can become valuable sources of buyer and seller referrals.
37
Approaching Strategic Buyers
Learn how to identify and approach companies that may benefit strategically from acquiring a business.
38
Approaching Financial Buyers, HNIs & Family Offices
Understand how to engage financial buyers who are seeking investment, cash flow or ownership opportunities.
39
Qualifying Buyers & Avoiding Time Wasters
Learn how to identify serious, credible buyers and avoid unproductive discussions.
40
Buyer Meetings & Discovery Conversations
Understand how to conduct professional buyer meetings and uncover motivation, capacity and fit.
41
Managing Buyer Objections & Concerns
Learn how to address common buyer questions and concerns without overselling or compromising confidentiality.
42
Buyer-Seller Matching & Deal Positioning
Learn how to assess fit and position a business opportunity for the right buyer.
43
Managing the Offer Process (EOI, LOI & Indicative Offers)
Understand how buyer interest progresses into structured offers and formal next-step commitments.
44
Negotiation Fundamentals for Business Brokers
Learn core negotiation principles and how brokers can help both parties move towards agreement.
45
Deal Structures – Cash Deals, Earn-Outs, Deferred Payments & Equity Swaps
Understand common deal structures and how payment terms affect risk, valuation and seller outcomes.
46
Due Diligence – Understanding the Buyer’s Investigation Process
Learn what buyers investigate before acquisition and how brokers can support an organised diligence process.
47
Data Rooms, Documentation Management & Information Requests
Understand how to manage documents, data rooms and buyer information requests securely and efficiently.
48
Purchase Agreements, Share Purchase Agreements (SPA) & Asset Purchase Agreements (APA)
Learn the purpose and key differences between major transaction agreements.
49
Transaction Closing, Handover & Success Fee Collection
Understand the final steps of closing, ownership handover and professional collection of brokerage fees.
50
Ethics, Professional Standards & Building a Long-Term Business Broking Career
Learn how to build a respected, sustainable broking career through ethics, competence, relationships and consistent execution.
